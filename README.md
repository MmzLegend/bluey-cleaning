# Bluey Cleaning Services

Responsive static website for Bluey Cleaning Services, Abuja, Nigeria.

## Contact

Phone and WhatsApp: +234 812 892 0329, confirmed by the owner of this project. The enquiry form opens WhatsApp with a prepared message; visitors review and send it themselves. No enquiry is stored by the site and no booking is automatically confirmed.

## Content

The service categories are an initial presentation of home, office, deep and move-in/move-out cleaning. Scope, pricing and availability are agreed directly with Bluey. The loss policy was confirmed by the project owner on 1 October 2026: if an item is taken and evidence shows it happened while the team was working in the customer’s space, Bluey will cover the loss. No insurance, certification, staff-training credentials, testimonials or customer-count claims are made. Staff training and supervision are topics to discuss before booking. The custom domain is blueycleaning.com.ng.

## Editing and hosting

Edit `index.html`, `style.css` and `script.js`. Original SVG illustrations are in `assets/`. Google Fonts supplies DM Sans and Manrope, with local sans-serif fallbacks. There are no build dependencies.

GitHub Pages publishes the root of the `main` branch. All site links and assets use relative URLs for project-page compatibility.

## WhatsApp and feedback

The floating widget opens a contact panel with a WhatsApp link. The feedback form supports an optional name and rating, feedback categories, required feedback text, and a copyable fallback. It prepares a private WhatsApp message; visitors review and send it themselves. Feedback is not stored or published on the site. Public testimonials require genuine customer reviews and permission to publish.
