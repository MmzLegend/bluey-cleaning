GOOD TO KNOW

you might ask.

How much will my clean cost?+

Your quote depends on the size and condition of the space and the tasks you need. Share the details with Bluey for a quote before booking.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?+

Ask when arranging your clean. We’ll agree what’s needed and who will provide it, including any product preferences or sensitive surfaces.

Can I arrange regular cleaning?+

Tell us your preferred frequency and days. Bluey can discuss a suitable arrangement, subject to availability.

Do you cover my area?+

We serve Abuja, Nigeria. Include your area in your enquiry so Bluey can confirm coverage and availability for your location.

How do I confirm a booking?+

Start with an enquiry below. Your booking is confirmed only after the service details, price and date have been agreed directly with Bluey.
